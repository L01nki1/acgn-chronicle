## Why this is a retrieval miss, not a data defect

Checked against the frozen snapshot and the index metadata:

- all 13 gold URLs are present in both e5 collections;
- all 13 carry a valid `published_ts` and fall inside their item `window`
  (`2023-10-28 .. 2026-10-28`), so the date filter is not excluding them;
- the returned top-5 are on-topic pages (ANN, Nijimen, RealSound, Chuapp) but not the gold page.

Examples:

| QA | Gold | Retrieved top-1 |
|---|---|---|
| A-901 | `corp.toei-anim.co.jp/ja/press/press-488412368085442252.html` | `nijimen.kusuguru.co.jp/topics/484283` |
| B-908 | `capcom.co.jp/ir/news/html/260907.html?utm_…` | ANN Onimusha article (2026-06-09) |
| B-909 | `capcom.co.jp/ir/news/html/260609.html?utm_…` | ANN live-action Street Fighter article (2026-10-19) |

This is consistent with #102: the limitation is cross-lingual representation
(English queries over Japanese evidence), not the threshold, `top_k`, or a URL form.

## Consequence for #111 §4.2

With a 0/13 baseline, bge-m3 displaces e5 only if it retrieves **≥2 of 13**.
The MRR dead-band cannot bind (baseline MRR is 0), so the rule reduces to the hit count.
The instrument remains coarse; it can separate 2+ from 0–1, nothing finer.
