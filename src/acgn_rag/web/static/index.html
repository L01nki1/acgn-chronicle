Contract

What this page guarantees
  • 01

    A citation without a URL is never rendered, greyed out or tucked into a tooltip.

  • 02

    Bodies are clipped once on the server and again before client rendering, at 200 characters.

  • 03

    Every result shows its snapshot; the cache key covers snapshot, question, filters, top_k and config.

  • 04

    410 means the snapshot is retired; the page never switches to the current one.

  • 05

    No excerpts is not a refusal; a refusal always states its reason.
